Kashmir is one of the most beautiful regions in India and is often known for its stunning natural scenery. It is surrounded by majestic mountains, green valleys, beautiful lakes, and peaceful landscapes. Srinagar is famous for Dal Lake, where visitors can enjoy traditional shikara rides and experience the beauty of the surrounding mountains. Gulmarg is well known for its green meadows, snowy mountains, and winter activities. Pahalgam is another popular destination, surrounded by forests, rivers, and scenic valleys. Sonamarg is famous for its snow-covered mountains and breathtaking views. Kashmir is also known for its gardens, traditional handicrafts, delicious food, and unique culture. The changing seasons make the region attractive throughout the year, with colorful flowers in spring, pleasant greenery in summer, golden landscapes in autumn, and snow-covered scenery in winter. Kashmir offers a peaceful environment and memorable experiences for people who enjoy nature, mountains, and beautiful landscapes.
